The koi pond's rain and fish make ripples in soft blobs among the cubes, or, with Water ripples on the Style tab, as columns of blocks that rise and fall in steps, see-through enough to show the koi under them (Ripple height sets how high they stand).
- Engine: `Actor.flows` marks a surface that moves by itself (a pond's is marked already); `BlockLook.flow` draws it as blocks or leaves it blobs, and `BlockLook.swell` sets how high its ripples stand.
