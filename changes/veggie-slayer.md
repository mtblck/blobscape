Veggie Slayer (unlisted while it is reviewed): a mini game of grumpy vegetables built from blocks. Tap them, or swipe through several at once for a combo, to burst them into a shower of cubes before they fall back down. Rare veggies are worth more, a pumpkin takes three hits, a golden Brussels sprout is worth 25, and hitting a treat costs one of your five hearts. The end shows how many of each you burst, and your best score stays on your device.
