- Diorama and Scene Creator have six new styles: Claymation (matte clay with thumbprints, moving in held frames like stop motion), Blueprint (white drafting lines on blue paper over a grid, with dimension lines), Thermal camera (fires and lamps run hot, the sky cold, in a heat camera's colors), Paper cutout (layers of cut paper each casting a soft shadow), Mosaic (small tiles set in grout) and CRT / VHS (scanlines, smeared color, wandering rows and a tracking band). Each has its own settings and takes the shared palette.
- Engine: `Stage.setFilter` draws a stage in any of the six (`FilterPass`, with the arithmetic in `splat-core/filters.ts` as its CPU twin), and `SplatWorld.posedBox(true)` gives the box of the subject alone.
