Pixel Diorama: weather and cloud cover for every scene. The Scene tab has Weather (rain, heavy rain, a thunderstorm with lightning, snow, heavy snow, fog) and Clouds (partly cloudy, cloudy, heavy clouds): the same recipes on every scene and in every style, with drops and flakes that land on the scene, a sky that turns gray, dimmer light and haze. Changing the weather eases the sky from one to the next without the scene being made again. The lighthouse and the campfire now take their moon, stars and clouds from the same shared sky, so clouds put the stars and then the moon out. Rain on a scene's water rings it, and the lighthouse's sea is now real water. In the Blocks style there is fog, and weather can be drawn as soft blobs or as blocks. Wind, from a breeze to a gale, comes with the weather or on its own: it blows rain and snow at a slant in gusts and raises waves on a scene's water, snow builds up over the scene while it falls and melts after, and a thunderstorm no longer stutters in the Blocks style.
